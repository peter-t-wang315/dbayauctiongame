# Sam notes
- Game started on my turn. Tip toast said bid cards don't score, only items do. I tapped Got it, so I never knew the tip count.
- Opening: $100 on Decanter, then $50/$125 on Tent. Lost both to higher bids. Cards come back when outbid (good), but I only noticed from the greyed-out/"out" state.
- Learned fast that bidding low on uncontested bays ($10, $25) wins stuff. Won Girls' Bicycle ($25), Meteorite ($10, a $270 steal), Tuba ($75 then Proxy) this way.
- Tried a Proxy on Tuba. It showed "Proxy" as my bid and I was "winning", but I did not understand what it beats or costs.
- Bid $200 on the wild Display Case and won it. Never got a Gift choice (Gift never went to me).
- Game was long (about 11 min). Halfway banner helped. Reveal screen: tap-through with ? chips then numbers. Final: 3rd place with $600.
## Verdict
$600, 3rd of 4 (Dev $2,055, Mia $1,380, Ray $385). Fun 6/10: bidding loop is snappy, but I had no sets and no big win moment.
## Best moments
- Meteorite est $150 revealed as $270 with a STEAL! badge.
- Cards returning to hand when outbid made fast re-bids easy.
- Halfway banner.
## Worst moments
- Reveal stage: I scrolled a long list of text, no drama for MY wins, no big "you won!" at the moment of sale.
- Seeing Dev's x2 Antiques = $510 and $480 (final screen Sam-092.png) and realising I spent my turns on random one-offs.
## Bugs / UI problems

- Display Case wild was not shown in my SETS strip text ("Media, Collectibles, Antiques" only) (Sam-082.png).
- Card indices shift after tip dismiss, easy to mis-tap on phone (Sam-006 to 008.png).
- Wait text at end of bid ("Bid placed, waiting...") sits next to second tip toast, cramped (Sam-010.png).
- Reveal "Skip" and long scroll; my own rows show "?" with no animation visible in static view (Sam-090.png).
## Did the game teach you the rules?
- First tip (bid cards are free/only items score) was clear and well-timed. Second tip about Gift appeared when I had no context.
- Sets x2: the SETS bar showed "3 = x2" and "Media 1 of 3", so I understood by turn 4. I did not manage to get one.
- Proxy: only learned by trying; no explanation of what it does in the UI I noticed.
- Gift: tip text understood but it never triggered for me.
- Winning an item was NOT clear or exciting. No fanfare at sale time.
## What I would have wanted
- Big "YOU WON!" pop with item art and sound/vibration when a bay closes in my favor.
- Clear indication in the reveal of what I won and why I ranked where I did.
- Faster pace; the 2:00 turn clock is huge but turns were fast anyway.
## Top 5 changes
1. Win-moment fanfare at sale time (toast + haptic + item flying into collection).
2. Explain Proxy inline when selected (one line: "matches the top bid, beats ties").
3. Keep card button positions stable when the tip toast dismisses.
4. Show wild cards in the SETS strip and what set they'd complete.
5. Shorter game option / animated reveal with running score bar instead of a wall of text.
