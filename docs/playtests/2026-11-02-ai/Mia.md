# Mia notes
- Started game; Sam went first. Tips popped up one at a time (Gift, "bid cards don't score", Proxy on tap). I dismissed the first with "Got it" and later ones came on their own.
- Played "pick card, tap bay" every turn. Bid a lot early ($150 Decanter, $100/$200 Tent). Lost the Decanter to Dev at $200. Won Tent ($200 card consumed).
- Realized only when a $ card "spent" that winning burns the card while outbid cards return. That is the real economy and I learned it by watching the card row, not a tip.
- Won Gift bay with $125 and then lost track: it scored nothing, and the Gift choice came much later ("GIFT TIME", pick Dev who had 9 items).
- Used Proxy on Vase and Doll; both worked. Got Walrus, Doll, E-bike ($300 for est $250), Polka Records, Snow Globe, Flamingo.
- Ran low on high cards by mid-game (only $10/$50/$75/$300 left), so I passed once and mostly bid cheap.
- Reveal screen showed hidden values vs estimate; Dev got huge multipliers (x2 antiques, STEALs) and won.
## Verdict
2nd place, $1,380 (Dev won with $2,055). Fun 7/10: fast turns and a fun reveal, but my choices felt like guessing.
## Best moments
- The reveal ("est $130 -> $210 STEAL!") was exciting.
- Proxy beating a top bid felt powerful.
## Worst moments
- Not knowing items' true value until the end, so spending $300 felt blind.
- Waiting while 3 others took turns (~10-20s each).
- Dev's x2 antiques set swung the game and I never saw it coming.
## Bugs / UI problems
- Bays are disabled until a card is selected, but no hint says "tap a card first" after the first tip is dismissed (Mia-024.png).
- No visible "you won X" banner at the moment of winning; I found out via the log line "Yours! Electric Bicycle" or the collections count (Mia-091.png, Mia-095.png).
- Cards {spent} vs {out} difference (spent forever vs returns) is not explained anywhere.
- Collections chip text is truncated in the accessible list; can't see set progress without opening it.
- My Teapot bid lost to Dev's $125 with no notification besides the log.
## Did the game teach you the rules?
Partly. The tips helped for Proxy and bid cards not scoring. I figured out sets of 3 = x2 only from the reveal screen ("x2 collection!"). Gift was explained but I did not understand the payoff until the end. Winning an item was not a clear, exciting moment; it was a quiet log line.
## What I would have wanted
A celebratory pop when I win a bay, a visible hint of how many cards I have left, set progress shown on the bay (e.g. "this is your 2nd Toys"), and some info on true value.
## Top 5 changes
1. Big "YOURS!" win moment with the item.
2. Explain spent vs returned cards in a tip.
3. Show category and set progress on each bay card.
4. Prompt "tap a card, then a bay" on first turn.
5. Shorter waits / quicker bot-like pace between turns.
