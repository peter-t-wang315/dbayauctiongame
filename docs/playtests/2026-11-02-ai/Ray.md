# Ray notes
- Waited in lobby; Mia started. Opening tip "Bid cards don't score: only the items you win do. Spend them!" appeared on my first turn.
- First turns I bid small ($25-$75) on a few bays; Mia and Sam kept outbidding me, my cards came back when outbid (nice, that clicked).
- Won Ab-Roller 3000 for $25 ("Yours!" in log) - the win was only a log line + toast, not exciting. Est $30 -> actual value $10, "Ripped off!" at reveal.
- Hesitated at bay decisions: I kept picking cards that were still "out" on another bay; pressing a number key for a used card silently did nothing and I lost a few seconds each time.
- Near the end I tried a $200 bid on the Comic Book; bay was "illegal: Proxy tops" (Dev's Proxy) - I did not understand a Proxy can't be beaten by a number; I passed.
- Reveal: my one real win, Bean-Bag Bear, was a STEAL ($100 -> $360). Others stacked 7-8 items each and sets of x2; I won only 3 items and finished last.
## Verdict
$385, 4th of 4. Fun 5/10: friendly and quick, but I felt I was always one step behind and didn't see how to compete with big bids.
## Best moments
- Reveal of the Bear: est $120 -> $360 STEAL.
- Cards returning when outbid.
## Worst moments
- Realizing at the end that others had 7-8 items and I had 3; I had been bidding too timidly and too late.
- "Can't bid: Proxy tops" with no explanation of how to answer it.
## Bugs / UI problems
- Number-key mapping is positional ($10 = 1 ... $300 = 0), while spent cards stay in the row; pressing the key for a used card gives no message (Ray-0xx during turns after the Tent bid).
- Bays showed "sold" for a moment before replacing, plus "empty" bays late game (Ray-111.png) leaving little to bid on.
- Reveal table (Ray-120.png) is long; other players' rows show many "not revealed yet" lines.
## Did the game teach you the rules?
Only the bid-card tip. I understood bid cards are free and only items score after the first outbid/win. Sets of 3 = x2 I only saw at reveal ("x2 collection!"); no hint during play. Proxy: I never used one and didn't learn what it does beyond that it beats numbers. Gift: I bid on it once, never learned what it gives. Winning an item was not exciting.
## Compared to the board game
Much faster and no money; missing the shared pot feel of going back and forth in turns, and I miss seeing what the item is worth before I bid (est is shown but inaccurate).
## What I would have wanted
A running score/value of what I hold, a hint when I could build a set, and a clear sound/animation when I win a bay.
## Top 5 changes
1. Celebrate a win (banner/sound) when an auction closes in my favor.
2. Explain Proxy in-turn (tooltip on "Proxy tops").
3. Mark used/out cards visibly and give a message on invalid key press.
4. Show "set progress" (2 of 3 Toys) and the x2 rule during play.
5. Hint that est values can be a steal or ripped off.
